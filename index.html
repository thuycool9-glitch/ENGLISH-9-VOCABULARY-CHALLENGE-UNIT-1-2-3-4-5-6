How to play

  • Classic Mode: answer all 100 questions in order. Correct = +10 points. Finish a Unit = bonus points.
  • Practice Mode: choose a Unit and the number of questions. You can redo your mistakes.
  • Challenge Mode: 20 seconds per question. Answer fast for a speed bonus and keep a streak for COMBO points!
  • Click an answer only once – you cannot change it. After each answer you see the meaning, IPA and an example.
  • Use the 🔊 button to turn sound on or off.
